Privacy Policy & Patron Data Governance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Registered Address: 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012

1. Atelier Information Gathering Protocols

Atelier patrons engaging BIAS COUTURE X through our salon consultation services provide personal identifiers including full legal names, residential addresses, digital contact credentials, and bespoke anatomical measurement dossiers. These discrete data assets are compiled solely to facilitate custom garment drafting, textile procurement schedules, and private Manhattan salon appointments. We operate with heightened discretion, treating all client anatomical records with the exact confidentiality standards expected of an international bespoke fashion house operating within the state of New York.

Automatic digital tracking mechanisms operating on our web platform gather standardized analytical telemetry including browser versions, operating systems, session timestamps, and geographic origin nodes. This analytical stream is processed in aggregated configurations without individual patron attribution to evaluate user engagement across our signature silhouette catalogs. Technical server logs are audited quarterly by our systems administration team to maintain security integrity, prevent malicious traffic infiltration, and verify that digital reservation forms transmit encrypted client inquiries directly to our concierge team at 181 Mercer Street.

2. Data Processing Architecture & Storage

All digital records collected during private salon appointments, bridal inquiries, and bespoke commission communications are stored on protected server environments utilizing multi-layered cryptographic safeguards and role-based access restrictions. Only credentialed atelier master drapers, executive directors, and concierge dispatchers possess administrative clearance to review patron anatomical specifications or fitting records. We enforce strict internal non-disclosure policies to ensure that client sizing details, custom pattern modifications, and event dates remain confidential throughout the entire lifespan of our commercial relationship.

We strictly prohibit the sale, monetization, commercial rental, or unauthorized external distribution of our client rosters to commercial marketing syndicates, external advertising networks, or third-party behavioral aggregators. Data transmissions to external service vendors are limited exclusively to secure credit card settlement providers and insured international shipping carriers responsible for white-glove gown delivery. Each external vendor contractually guarantees full compliance with United States federal data privacy statutes, international data processing benchmarks, and our institutional confidentiality mandates.

3. Patron Privacy Rights & Global Recourse

Patrons retain full rights under applicable state, federal, and international statutory privacy frameworks to inspect, update, or permanently expunge their personal records from our salon archives. You may request an audited summary of all personal information held in our active databases by transmitting an authorized written inquiry to our executive management office. We process authenticated data disclosure and deletion requests within thirty business days, ensuring our archives reflect your current preferences while preserving statutory tax records required under United States commercial code.

Should you wish to modify your communication permissions, update your primary salon delivery location, or submit a formal data privacy inquiry, our administrative team is directly accessible at our primary salon located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. Telephone inquiries regarding corporate data protection policies may be directed to our direct client desk at +1-888-777-5845. We maintain unyielding dedication to safeguarding the personal dignity, confidential records, and trusted patronage of every client who enters our doors.
