The 181 Mercer Protocol

Five Steps from Muslin Diagnostic to Red Carpet

I

Anatomical Consultation

Initial private appointment at 181 Mercer Street analyzing bone structure, gait mechanics, posture, and fabric weight preferences.

II

Toile Muslin Draping

Direct three-dimensional pattern sculpting in cotton muslin cut strictly on the bias to establish grain angles and drape apex points.

III

Silk Grain Suspension

Silk assembly followed by 72-hour gravitational relaxation on dressmaker mannequins before any hem or armhole measurements are taken.

IV

Final Hand Finishes

Hand-rolled continuous hems, silk thread loops, concealed bias plackets, and bespoke salon presentation at 181 Mercer Street.
