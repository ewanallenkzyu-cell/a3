Cookie & Digital Telemetry Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Registered Address: 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012

1. Categorization & Functional Use of Cookies

The digital platforms of BIAS COUTURE X utilize minimal, privacy-centric HTTP cookies and local storage tokens to ensure flawless browser navigation, responsive layout delivery, and secure consultation form processing. Cookies represent compact text files deposited upon your local terminal device that enable our servers to remember your preferred catalog viewing filters, temporary session states, and mobile drawer configurations. These fundamental technical tokens do not execute autonomous computer programs, transmit destructive code, or access confidential files stored within your private operating system.

We classify our digital cookies into two transparent operational categories: strictly necessary functional tokens and aggregated performance analytics markers. Essential functional tokens manage secure communication with our 181 Mercer Street appointment database, authenticate user submissions, and prevent cross-site request forgery attacks during consultation scheduling. Analytical tokens, including Google Analytics Tag G-0LY0HY7L01, gather anonymous telemetry regarding page response speeds, viewport resolutions, and content popularity to guide our continuing optimization of our digital haute couture salon presentation.

2. Third-Party Telemetry & Analytics Management

Analytical telemetry compiled through our digital properties is processed in anonymized configurations that mask individual Internet Protocol addresses before long-term statistical storage. We do not permit external advertising networks to deploy behavioral tracking pixels, commercial retargeting beacons, or invasive fingerprinting scripts across our website. The insights generated through our analytical telemetry are utilized strictly by our in-house creative directors and software engineers to verify that our high-resolution imagery and treatises load with lightning efficiency worldwide.

Patrons possess complete autonomy to accept, reject, or selectively restrict cookie storage through their chosen web browser preferences or device security control panels. Popular browsers including Safari, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge provide comprehensive preference menus allowing users to delete existing cookies or reject incoming tokens permanently. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact the interactivity of our appointment booking dossier or require manual re-selection of catalog filters during repeated salon website visits.

3. Policy Updates & Institutional Inquiries

We review our digital cookie governance protocols periodically to reflect updates in international data privacy statutes, browser security standards, and evolving web technologies. Any material modifications to our digital telemetry practices will be formally documented on this page alongside an updated effective calendar date. Your continued engagement with our web platform following statutory policy publications indicates your informed comprehension and ongoing consent to our updated technical cookie governance standards across all operating sessions.
